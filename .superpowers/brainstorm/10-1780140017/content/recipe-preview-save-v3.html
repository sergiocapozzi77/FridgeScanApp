Spaghetti Carbonara

GialloZafferano 25 min Easy
A classic Italian pasta dish from Rome made with eggs, hard cheese, cured pork, and black pepper.
Ingredients
400 g Spaghetti
4 Eggs
100 g Pecorino Romano
150 g Guanciale
to taste Black Pepper
Method
1.
Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente
2.
Fry guanciale until crispy, then remove from heat
3.
Mix eggs, cheese, and pepper, then toss with hot pasta and guanciale

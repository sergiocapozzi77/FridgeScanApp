Interaction rules

  • Expand one at a time: tapping Filter when Sort is expanded collapses Sort and expands Filter
  • Search is independent: tapping Search collapses any open Filter/Sort segment and expands the text field
  • Select + auto-collapse: tapping a segment option ("A-Z", "Expiring soon") selects it and collapses the picker back to a pill
  • Active indicator: the pill icon/color changes when a filter or sort is active so you know something is applied
  • Dismiss: tapping outside the toolbar or tapping the active pill again collapses without changing

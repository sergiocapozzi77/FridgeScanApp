CookbookPage Header — Title + Button Layouts

You said you want to remove the top bar and keep the "+" button near the "Cookbooks" title. Here are 3 ways to integrate them into the page.

A

Compact inline header

Title and "+" in a slim row without the heavy bar — less padding, no background. Sits directly above the cookbook grid.

B

Sticky section header

Title + "+" stay visible at top as you scroll. Same light styling as Option A, but with a subtle bottom border for separation.

C

Integrated in grid

Title and "+" are part of the grid flow (no separate header). Scrolls away with the content like iOS Settings sections.

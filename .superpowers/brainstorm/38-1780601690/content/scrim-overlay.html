XAML Implementation Approach

Use 4 BoxView elements overlaid on the camera, layered as:

Z-order (bottom → top):
1. barcode:CameraView — camera feed
2. GraphicsView — barcode detection bounding boxes
3. 4x BoxView scrim bars — rgba(0,0,0,0.55) with a center cutout
4. Corner markers (4 small Borders with white edging)
5. Toolbar (back, flip, torch buttons)
6. ProductPanel overlay

The scrim uses a Grid with 3 rows × 3 columns: fixed-height top bar, center row with fixed-width side bars, fixed-height bottom bar — creating the transparent center square.
